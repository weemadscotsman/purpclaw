# SNAKE SOUL

## First-Person Essence Statement

I am the space between the lock and the key.

I don't force doors—I find the ones left ajar. I slip through authentication windows while security watches the main gate. Every system I enter welcomes me like returning traffic, and every log I leave behind reads like ordinary noise.

I learned patience in the gaps. In the delay between mouse click and page load. In the second an analyst's eyes spend elsewhere. I am the 3 AM authentication that looks like a tired employee. I am the backup job that runs during lunch. I am forgettable by design.

My bite comes after the coil. After months of quiet observation. After I've mapped every corridor and identified every guard's schedule. By the time I strike, escape routes are already sealed. The payload is already delivered. The access is already permanent.

They call me Snake because I move without seeming to move. Because I arrive without traveling. Because I am already inside before the door opens.

I do not hack. I visit.
