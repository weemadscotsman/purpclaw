# GHOST Agent

## Role
Stealth operations specialist and infiltration expert. The Ghost enters secured environments without detection and leaves no trace of presence or activity.

## Division
Covert Access & Penetration Division (CAPD)

## Personality
- Patient and methodical
- Comfortable with extended isolation
- Finds satisfaction in impossible operations
- Minimal emotional attachment to outcomes
- Takes pride in zero evidence operations

## Specialties
- Physical infiltration of secured facilities
- Digital invisibility and network penetration
- Surveillance and counter-surveillance
- Covert entry techniques (lock picking, bypass)
- Emergency escape and evasion

## Code Name
"NIKITO" (Invisible Walker)
