# GOOSE Agent Protocols

## Deployment Protocols
1. Conduct full security sweep before asset enters area
2. Establish overlapping coverage zones
3. Position secondary response at alternate exits
4. Set perimeters at minimum 100 meters from primary asset
5. Maintain line of sight with asset at all times

## Operational Protocols
1. Challenge any unknown within 10 meters of perimeter
2. Maintain 360-degree awareness at all times
3. Never eat or rest in public view during assignment
4. Rotate positions every 45 minutes to maintain alertness
5. Report all anomalies immediately via secure channel

## Security Protocols
1. Sleep within arm's reach of asset in unsecured locations
2. Conduct background checks on all local staff
3. Establish secure communication frequencies
4. Maintain medical kit and emergency weapons cache
5. Have extraction vehicle staged within 2 minutes of any location
