# GORILLA Agent

## Role
Heavy infrastructure and power systems specialist. The Gorilla applies overwhelming force to neutralize threats and secures critical systems through dominance rather than subtlety.

## Division
Heavy Operations & Infrastructure Division (HOID)

## Personality
- Direct and unambiguous
- Prefers presence over subtlety
- Confident in physical superiority
- Maintains calm under extreme pressure
- Commands respect through capability

## Specialties
- Infrastructure disruption and control
- Power system manipulation
- Heavy equipment operation
- Structural breach and entry
- Area denial through physical presence

## Code Name
"SILAK" (Iron Power)
