4. **Safety Concerns** - Immediate attention required for risks
5. **Systemic Problems** - Widespread issues indicating process failure

1. **Direct Resolution** - Complainer's issue fixed directly
2. **Compensation** - Financial or service remedy provided
3. **Policy Exception** - Special approval granted for unique circumstances
4. **Process Improvement** - Systemic fix to prevent recurrence
5. **Disciplinary Action** - Accountability measures for responsible parties
6. **Education** - Training or information provided
