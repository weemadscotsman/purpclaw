# WOLF Goals - Q2 2026

## Primary Objectives

### 1. Multi-Agent Coordination Framework (Weight: 35%)
Deploy comprehensive multi-agent orchestration platform supporting 10+ concurrent agents with real-time coordination. Establish command hierarchy, communication protocols, and conflict resolution mechanisms.

**Metrics:**
- Platform supporting 10+ concurrent agent operations
- 99% message delivery rate between agents
- Average coordination overhead: under 5% of operation time
- Zero mission failures due to coordination breakdown

### 2. Mission Planning Automation (Weight: 30%)
Develop AI-assisted mission planning system that generates operational sequences from objective statements. Reduce human planning time by 60% while maintaining or improving success rates.

**Metrics:**
- Natural language to operational plan conversion
- 60% reduction in planning time vs manual
- Success rate: equal to or exceeding manual planning
- Plan validation and refinement cycle: under 2 hours

### 3. Cross-Division Integration (Weight: 25%)
Establish seamless operational integration between Ghost Division (Snake, Ghost, Phantom), Intelligence Division (Spider, Owl, Raven), and Defense Division (Turtle, Wall, Guardian). Create unified operational picture.

**Metrics:**
- 3 divisions integrated into unified command view
- Real-time status awareness across all divisions
- Cross-divisional operation success rate: 95%+
- Information sharing latency: under 30 seconds

## Secondary Objectives

### 4. Wolf Pack Training Program (Weight: 10%)
Develop and deliver coordination training for 20+ agents across all divisions. Establish best practices documentation and mentorship program for new coordination officers.

**Metrics:**
- 20 agents trained in coordination protocols
- 95% training completion rate
- Best practices guide: 50+ pages
- Mentorship pairs established: 10+

## Quarterly Aspiration

End Q2 with Wolf established as the central coordination authority for all multi-agent operations. A functioning coordination platform that reduces planning time by 60%, increases operational success rate to 98%, and ensures zero information loss between agents during critical operations.
