FUNGUS AMONGUS, in normal words:

LIVEFORGE makes the active screens, forms, dashboards, mini-tools, and temporary workspaces.

Fungus Amongus is the underground mycelium that connects everything.

When any agent, tool, or LIVEFORGE surface discovers something useful, it creates a structured packet called a spore.

A spore says:
- what happened
- who saw it
- where the proof is
- who is allowed to know it
- how confident it is
- when it expires
- whether it can become a reusable pattern

The mycelium stores those spores, searches them, and sends compact nutrient bundles to agents when they need help.

So if one agent learns a better route, hits a tool failure, finds a useful pattern, or gets corrected by the user, the next relevant agent can benefit from that without blindly dumping all memory into every task.

Main safety rule:

Tells all, knows all, shares all only through scope, proof, and permission.

No secret spreading. No fake knowing. No permanent learning without replay.
