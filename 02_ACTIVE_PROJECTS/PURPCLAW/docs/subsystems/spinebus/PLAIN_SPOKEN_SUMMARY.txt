SPINEBUS is the next layer after LIVEFORGE Phase One.

LIVEFORGE stores surfaces, events, and receipts.

Fungus Amongus spreads useful proof-backed knowledge through the stack.

SPINEBUS is the traffic controller.

Every chat or job becomes a job envelope.

That envelope touches memory, mycelium, tower, skills, tools, execution gate, receipts, truth audit, autolearn, and dream mode.

Touch does not mean execute.

Touch means the subsystem gets a chance to add context, warn, route, block, or pass.

Only the selected agent, selected skills, and selected tools get to execute.

Every route writes receipts.

Every useful improvement becomes a lesson proposal.

When the user is inactive, dream mode can inspect receipts and propose faster, cleaner, cheaper tool routes.

Dream mode must not push to main or mutate the system without approval.

Phase Two should build the route planner and proof lane, not full automation.

The win is simple: PURPCLAW stops guessing which agent, skill, or tool to use. It routes from evidence.
